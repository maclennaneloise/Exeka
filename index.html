The problem

Planning in silos costs more.

Bulk fabric, bigger runs and bulk freight each look cheapest on their own. Together, they can leave you with excess stock, unused fabric and cash tied up instead of funding the next collection.

Hidden costs

Storage, markdowns, leftover fabric and cash tied up rarely make it into the decision.

Siloed decisions

Fabric, production and freight are each chosen on price, not on their combined cost.

One plan at a time

Spreadsheets test the plan already chosen, not every alternative.

Founder-dependent

Trade-offs are made by intuition, and lessons from past orders aren't captured.

Capabilities

The planning power of an ERP, without the ERP.

Document capture

Reads quotes, tech packs and supplier emails, so there's no data entry.

E.g. A fabric quote is uploaded; the price breaks, MOQ and lead time are extracted for confirmation.

Scenario modelling

Builds every combination of fabric, quantity, freight and timing.

E.g. Two styles share a linen; Exeka combines them to meet the MOQ without excess fabric.

Total cost analysis

Adds up every cost a plan causes, now and later, across low, likely and high sales.

E.g. A bulk order saves $2 a metre but leaves 70 dresses to store and mark down.

Optimal plan

Recommends the lowest-total-cost plan and explains what would change it.

E.g. "Make 160, not 240. Above 200 expected sales, the bulk order becomes optimal."

Why now

AI makes integrated planning affordable.

The methods larger brands use have existed for decades, but needed analysts and clean data. AI reads the documents founders already have and explains results in plain language, so small brands can plan this way too.

Case study / 01

ArtClub x CANVS Bottega

Took a sustainable tracksuit collection from sourcing local production partners through ordering, stock and fulfilment, and saw how production decisions depend on the founder piecing information together.

Read more

At ArtClub, the independent fashion and art business founded by Heidi Middleton, I worked on the production and launch of a sustainable tracksuit collection in collaboration with CANVS Bottega.

I supported the collection from initial concept through to fulfilment: sourcing local, sustainable production partners and packaging, placing orders, coordinating stock across both brands' ecommerce sites, and fulfilling customer orders.

Opportunities I observed

  • Founder-led decisions: critical product and production decisions relied on bringing the right information to the founder at the right time.
  • Information across systems: product, supplier and cost information naturally lived across emails, spreadsheets and other sources.
  • Joint-venture complexity: the collaboration introduced another layer of costs, suppliers, stock and coordination alongside the core business.
  • Untapped commerce data: Shopify held valuable sales and customer signals with the potential to inform more product and commercial decisions.

Case study / 02

The Bower Reuse & Repair Centre

Mapped workshop operations for a volunteer-led not-for-profit and introduced simple controls, automations and a shared source of truth to make growth manageable.

Read more

With workshop sign-ups and revenue below previous levels, I worked with The Bower, a volunteer-led not-for-profit, to understand how its workshop programme could support future growth.

I interviewed the volunteer team and a third-party booking partner, analysed customer feedback, and mapped the end-to-end workshop operation and customer journey. Interest already existed from schools and community groups, but a small volunteer team was managing scheduling, facilitators, booking platforms and customer communications. We prioritised strengthening the operation first, so future marketing and partnerships had a stronger foundation.

What I implemented

  • One shared source of truth for workshop schedules and readiness
  • Simple operational controls around readiness and cancellations
  • Monday.com automations for time-sensitive actions
  • Clear work instructions for recurring processes
  • Shared collaboration tools designed around a small volunteer team
